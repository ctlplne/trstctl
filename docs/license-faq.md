# License FAQ

This page explains trstctl's license in plain language. The
[LICENSE](https://github.com/ctlplne/trstctl/blob/main/LICENSE) file is the binding
text; where this page and the LICENSE differ, the LICENSE wins. For anything this
page doesn't answer, write to licensing@trstctl.com.

## Can my organization run trstctl in production for free?

Yes. The core runs in production at no charge, with no signed license and no meter,
whatever your size. Your affiliates (organizations with more than 50% common
ownership with yours), your employees, and contractors who work as part of your
staff all count as you.

## We already use other vendors' tools, including competing ones. Do we have to replace them?

No. Using trstctl alongside other vendors' tools, including competing ones, is
allowed; you don't need to replace anything. You can move one area at a time, for
example certificate lifecycle first and privileged access later, and connect
trstctl to the tools you keep.

## Can a consultant or service provider set it up for us?

Yes. One-off project work on your own infrastructure (installing, configuring,
upgrading, migrating or troubleshooting your deployment) is allowed without a
commercial license, as long as you keep sole administrative control of the
deployment afterwards.

## Can a service provider run it for us on an ongoing basis?

Only with a Provider license. Running trstctl for a customer as an ongoing service,
on the customer's infrastructure or the provider's, and hosting it for a customer
both need one.

## Can we use it to secure products and services we sell?

Yes. Issuing credentials to devices or software you supply to your customers, or to
partners that connect to your own services, is allowed, as long as you don't sell
certificate, secret, key or identity management to them as a service.

## Can we build it into a product we sell?

Yes, unless that product's main purpose is managing certificates, secrets,
cryptographic keys, SSH credentials, privileged accounts, or workload or machine
identities. Building trstctl into that kind of product needs a commercial license.

## Can we offer trstctl to others as a hosted or managed service?

Not under the free license. That is what the Provider license is for.

## Can we rebrand it?

In production, keep the trstctl name, logos and "Powered by trstctl" attribution
visible. You can show your own name and logo alongside them. The Provider edition's
white label adds your brand for each customer and keeps "Powered by trstctl" on
every screen, email and report.

## Can we change the code, test it, or use it for development?

Yes. Copying, modifying and redistributing the code, and any non-production use,
are always allowed.

## What happens after four years?

Each published version of the core becomes available under the Mozilla Public
License 2.0 four years after it is published.

## What about versions published before this license text?

Each keeps the license it was published with.
